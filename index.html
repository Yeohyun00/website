I am Yeohyun Jung (정여현), and my interest lies at the intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) and well-being. I passionately study and design technologies that support user's health and well-being while preserving positive user experiences.

I aim to bridge the gap between evolving technology and real-world being, contributing to find the right balance by listening to users and understanding what they truly need.

I received my master's degree in Industrial Design at KAIST. I worked as the member of DxD Lab, under the supervision of Professor Hwajung Hong. Before that, I received my bachelor's degree in Psychology from Yonsei University.
